# AWS Elastic Disaster Recovery

> [!note] Exam scope
> Not on AWS's in-scope or out-of-scope list. The in-scope list is non-exhaustive, so treat this as background.

Continuously replicates servers into a low-cost staging area in AWS and launches them within minutes during a disaster. It gives a pilot light style setup with seconds of [[RPO and RTO|RPO]].
