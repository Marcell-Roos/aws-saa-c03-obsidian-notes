# RDS Monitoring

**[[Amazon RDS|RDS]] Enhanced Monitoring** - provides detailed **real-time** insights into the OS metrics of our RDS instances. It helps us monitor the performance of our RDS instances more effectively than basic monitoring alone.

**[[Amazon CloudWatch|CloudWatch]] Database Insights** replaced RDS Performance Insights, which reached end of life on 31 July 2026. It gives a higher-level view of how queries and workloads perform over time.
