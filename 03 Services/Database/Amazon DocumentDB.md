# Amazon DocumentDB

**DocumentDB** is “AWS implementation” of MongoDB. It has similar deployment concepts as [[Amazon Aurora|Aurora]] (fully managed, highly available with replication across 3 AZ). Its storage automatically grows in increments of 10GB.

Automatically scales to workloads with millions of requests per second.
