# RDS Blue-Green Deployments

Creates a synchronised staging copy of production so you can upgrade engines or change schema, then switch over with little downtime. Supported on [[Amazon RDS|RDS]] for MariaDB, MySQL and PostgreSQL, and on [[Amazon Aurora|Aurora]].
