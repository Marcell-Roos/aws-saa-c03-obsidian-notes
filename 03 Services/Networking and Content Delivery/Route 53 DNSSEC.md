# Route 53 DNSSEC

DNSSEC signs [[DNS Fundamentals|DNS]] records, so resolvers can detect tampering or spoofing. [[Amazon Route 53|Route 53]] supports signing for public hosted zones and validation through [[Route 53 Resolver]].
