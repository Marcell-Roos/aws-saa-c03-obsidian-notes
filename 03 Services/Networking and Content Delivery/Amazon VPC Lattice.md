# Amazon VPC Lattice

> [!note] Exam scope
> Not on AWS's in-scope or out-of-scope list. The in-scope list is non-exhaustive, so treat this as background.

Managed application networking to connect, secure and monitor services across [[Amazon VPC|VPCs]] and accounts, with no need for peering or a [[Elastic Load Balancing|load balancer]] per service.
