# Amazon Macie

**Amazon Macie** is a fully managed data security and data privacy service that uses ML and pattern matching to discover and protect our sensitive data in AWS. Macie helps identify and alert us to sensitive data in our **[[Amazon S3|S3]] Buckets**, such as personally identifiable information (PII).
