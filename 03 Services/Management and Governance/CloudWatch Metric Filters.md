# CloudWatch Metric Filters

Turn log patterns into metrics, such as counting `ERROR` lines, so an alarm can act on them. A **subscription filter** instead streams matching logs to [[Kinesis Data Streams]], [[Amazon Data Firehose]] or [[AWS Lambda]].
