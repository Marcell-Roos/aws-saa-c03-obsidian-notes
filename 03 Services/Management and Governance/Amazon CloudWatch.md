# Amazon CloudWatch

Monitoring for AWS resources and apps: metrics, logs, alarms and dashboards. By default [[Amazon EC2|EC2]] reports metrics every 5 minutes, or every minute with detailed monitoring. RAM isn't reported without the [[CloudWatch Agent]].
