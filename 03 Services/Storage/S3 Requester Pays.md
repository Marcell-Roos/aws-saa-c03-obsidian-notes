# S3 Requester Pays

In general, buckets owners pay for all [[Amazon S3|S3]] storage and data transfer costs associated with their buckets. With Requester Pays buckets, instead of the bucket owner, the requester pays the cost of the request and the data download from the bucket.

The requester must be authenticated in AWS.
