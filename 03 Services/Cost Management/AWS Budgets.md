# AWS Budgets

Sets cost, usage, or [[Savings Plans]] and [[EC2 Reserved Instances|Reserved Instance]] targets, and alerts when actual or **forecasted** values cross a threshold. Budget actions can apply [[IAM Policy Structure|IAM policies]] or [[Service Control Policies|SCPs]], or stop instances. Pick it for "notify me when spend reaches X". [[AWS Cost Explorer]] is for analysis.
