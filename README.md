# AWS SAA-C03 Obsidian Study Vault

An [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) vault for the **AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate (SAA-C03)** exam. It is a complete course: 31 study chapters in order, a step-by-step study guide, 11 hands-on labs, more than 270 chapter self-test questions, and five full-length practice exams.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This vault is AI-generated.** It was written and assembled by Claude, Anthropic's AI model, from the sources below. No person has reviewed it line by line, so it may contain mistakes. No study material can guarantee a pass. AWS recommends about a year of hands-on experience for this exam.

Key facts were checked against AWS documentation on 1 November 2026, and reviewed again on 2 November 2026. AWS changes quickly, so some details will go out of date. [`Corrections Log`](04%20Reference/Corrections%20Log.md) records what was checked, and what was not.

## What's inside

| Folder | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `00 Study Guide` | Step-by-step plan, with paces from 5 to 16 weeks, a readiness gate and a mistake log |
| `01 Exam` | Exam overview, four domain pages covering every exam guide item, exam technique and a final cheat sheet |
| `02 Topics` | 31 chapters in study order. Each ends with exam patterns and a self-test |
| `03 Practice` | Five 65-question practice exams with explanations, and hands-on labs |
| `04 Reference` | Index of all 119 in-scope services, lifecycle status, sources, corrections and licences |

## How to use it

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. In Obsidian, choose **Open folder as vault** and select it.
3. Open `Home`, then follow the `Study Guide`.

`04 Reference/How to Use This Vault` explains the layout, tags and graph filters.

## How well it matches the exam

- The domains, task statements and in-scope and out-of-scope lists come from AWS's exam guide v1.1, dated 29 October 2026.
- Each of the 189 knowledge and skill items in the guide is mapped to a chapter section on the domain pages.
- Practice questions are original, and are written for this vault. They are not real exam questions, and their difficulty has not been calibrated against the real exam. Real questions are often longer. Exam 5 is the closest in style.
