# OLTP and OLAP

- **OLTP:** many small transactions on current data, row-oriented. Use [[Amazon RDS]], [[Amazon Aurora]] or [[Amazon DynamoDB]].
- **OLAP:** few large analytical queries over history, columnar. Use [[Amazon Redshift]] or [[Amazon Athena]].
