# Serverless Patterns

No servers to manage, automatic scaling, and pay for use. Common building blocks are [[AWS Lambda]], [[AWS Fargate]], [[Amazon API Gateway]], [[Amazon DynamoDB]], [[Amazon S3]], [[Amazon SQS]], [[Amazon EventBridge]] and [[AWS Step Functions]].

Choose containers over [[AWS Lambda|Lambda]] when a job runs longer than 15 minutes, needs a long-lived process, or has a heavy runtime. See [[Compute Selection Guide]].
